## Otter vs Fathom vs Fireflies: Head-to-Head

### Otter.ai

[Otter.ai](/tools/otter-ai) is the familiar choice for searchable meeting transcripts. It handles Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams, produces speaker-labeled notes, and makes past conversations easy to search — valuable for journalists, researchers, PMs doing discovery, and anyone who needs **verbatim recall**.

**Strengths:** Search, sharing, live transcription, mobile app, interview workflows.

**Weaknesses:** Summaries are useful but less sales-optimized than Fathom or Fireflies; CRM depth is limited.

**Pick Otter if:** you run customer interviews, user research, or internal meetings where quote accuracy matters more than pipeline automation.

### Fathom

[Fathom](/tools/fathom) has won mindshare among founders and sales teams for a simple reason: a **generous free tier** and summaries that map cleanly to CRM follow-ups. It records calls (with consent), highlights action items, and integrates with HubSpot and Salesforce on paid plans.

**Strengths:** Free tier, clean UX, CRM-friendly output, low friction for small teams.

**Weaknesses:** Less depth for enterprise conversation intelligence than Fireflies or Gong.

**Pick Fathom if:** you are a startup doing sales and customer calls and want notes → CRM without enterprise pricing.

### Fireflies.ai

[Fireflies.ai](/tools/fireflies-ai) sits between Otter's general transcription and dedicated revenue intelligence platforms. It joins meetings, transcribes, summarizes, and offers topic tracking, sentiment hints, and broad CRM integrations — popular with sales and customer success teams.

**Strengths:** CRM integrations, team libraries, conversation search, coaching features on higher tiers.

**Weaknesses:** Can feel heavy for teams that only need simple notes; UI density varies by plan.

**Pick Fireflies if:** you want meeting notes **and** sales-call analytics without jumping to enterprise-only tools.

## Other Strong Options

### tl;dv

[tl;dv](/tools/tl-dv) is popular with product and UX teams for timestamped clips, highlight reels, and sharing key moments from long calls. Less sales-CRM native; stronger for research synthesis.

### Read AI

[Read AI](/tools/read-ai) aggregates meeting summaries across your calendar and can extend into email and messaging context — useful when meetings are only part of the communication overload.

### Granola

[Granola](/tools/granola) takes a lighter approach: notes without a visible bot in the call, appealing to teams sensitive to participant experience.

### Motion

[Motion](/tools/motion) bundles calendar, task management, and meeting notes for individuals who want scheduling and notes in one subscription.

## Sales Teams: Notes vs Revenue Intelligence

Meeting notes tools capture what was said. **Revenue intelligence** tools like [Gong](/tools/gong) analyze call patterns, deal risk, and coaching at scale across a sales org.

If you are a five-person startup, Fathom or Fireflies is usually enough. If you are a 50-person sales team with forecast accountability, evaluate Gong alongside Fireflies — they solve overlapping but not identical problems.

Support teams evaluating AI should also compare [Zendesk AI](/tools/zendesk-ai) and [Intercom Fin](/tools/intercom-fin) for ticket-facing automation, not just internal meeting notes.

## Privacy and Consent

Meeting notes tools process sensitive data: pricing, hiring, health, customer complaints, strategy.

Before rolling out any tool:

1. **Tell participants** recordings are happening — bot join or verbal notice.
2. **Check retention settings** — who can access notes after someone leaves the company?
3. **Review vendor policies** — especially for regulated industries. See our [AI privacy guide](/blog/ai-privacy-guide-for-normal-people).
4. **Avoid pasting secrets** into AI summaries you do not control.

Tools differ on EU data handling, SSO, and admin controls. Enterprise buyers should verify DPA availability before procurement.

## Pricing Overview

Free tiers exist on Otter, Fathom, Fireflies, and tl;dv — sufficient for solo evaluation. Team plans typically run **$10–30/user/month** depending on integrations, storage, and intelligence features. Enterprise and revenue-intelligence platforms cost more.

Start free, run ten real meetings, then upgrade the team that actually uses the output.

## FAQ

### Otter vs Fathom — which is better?

Otter is better for general transcription and search across many meeting types. Fathom is better for sales calls and CRM-friendly summaries, especially on a budget.

### Is Fireflies better than Otter for sales?

Generally yes — Fireflies offers deeper CRM integrations and sales-oriented analytics. Otter is stronger for interview-style accuracy and simple search.

### Do these tools work with Zoom and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Otter, Fathom, Fireflies, tl;dv, and Read AI support major platforms; verify your exact plan and bot-join requirements.

### Are AI meeting notes HIPAA compliant?

Some vendors offer BAAs on enterprise plans; none of the consumer tiers are automatically HIPAA-ready. Verify with the vendor and your legal team before clinical use.

### Can I use meeting notes tools without a bot joining the call?

Some tools (Granola, local recording uploads) reduce visible bot presence. Most auto-join bots require participant consent policies.

### Fathom vs Fireflies for a small sales team?

Fathom for simplicity and cost. Fireflies if you need broader CRM support and a growing call library with analytics.
