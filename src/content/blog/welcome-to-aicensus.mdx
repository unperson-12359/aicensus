We built AiCensus because the AI tools landscape is noisy. Thousands of tools launch, rebrand, pivot, and vanish. Most directories either scrape everything or rank whoever pays the most.

AiCensus is meant to be simpler: a curated, human-readable map of useful AI tools.

## The Directory

AiCensus tracks 200+ AI tools across categories like chatbots, coding, image generation, video, productivity, research, agents, and infrastructure. Each listing focuses on what a tool does, who it is for, how pricing generally works, and what tradeoffs to expect.

You can filter by category, pricing model, verified status, and search terms. If you are looking for the right tool for a specific job, start on the [Browse page](/tools).

## Comparisons and Stacks

Finding one tool is only half the job. Most people need to understand how tools compare, which ones overlap, and what belongs in a real workflow.

That is why AiCensus includes:

- [Side-by-side comparisons](/compare) for popular tool pairs.
- [Curated stacks](/stacks) for common workflows.
- [Best-of guides](/best) for focused buying decisions.
- A [prompt builder](/prompt-builder) for turning rough tasks into better prompts.

## What Comes Next

We are keeping the product focused for now: better listings, better comparisons, better guides, and cleaner ways to understand the AI market.
