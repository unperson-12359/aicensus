We built AiCensus because the AI tools landscape is a mess. Thousands of tools launch every month. Most of them sound the same. And if you're actually building with these tools, there's nowhere good to show your work.

So we made a platform that solves both problems.

## The Directory

AiCensus has a curated directory of 150+ AI tools across 20+ categories. Every tool is reviewed by our team — no pay-to-play rankings, no fake reviews, no SEO spam. Just honest descriptions, real pricing info, and useful comparisons.

You can filter by category, pricing model, and verified status. If you're looking for the right tool for a specific job, start on the [Browse page](/tools).

## The Portfolio

This is the part we're most excited about.

If you've built something with an AI tool — a website with Bolt, an app with Cursor, a side project with Lovable, a quick experiment with Base44 — you can create a free portfolio on AiCensus to showcase it.

You get a public profile page at `aicensus.co/portfolio/your-username` with:

- Your bio and social links
- A gallery of your projects with live previews
- A contact form so visitors can reach out directly

No domain to buy. No hosting to set up. No audience required.

The idea is simple: upload your projects, get real eyes on them, and if people keep reaching out about something you built — that's your signal to take it seriously.

## What's Next

We're just getting started. In the coming months we're adding community features, tool comparison tools, and more ways for builders to get discovered.

For now, [send us what you've built](/contact) and start showing your best AI projects to more people.
