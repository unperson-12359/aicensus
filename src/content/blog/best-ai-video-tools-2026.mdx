## Text-to-Video Generators

These tools create video clips from text prompts or reference images. Clip length is usually short (5–25 seconds depending on the model); you stitch in an editor for longer pieces.

### Runway

[Runway](/tools/runway) remains the professional pick for generative video. The current flagship is **Gen-4.5** (Gen-3 is long retired), and a subscription now also unlocks third-party models like Google's Veo and ByteDance's Seedance inside the same workspace, plus Aleph for prompt-based editing of existing footage. Motion brush, camera controls, and director-style tooling make it the tool filmmakers and agencies reach for when they need iterative creative control — not a one-off novelty clip.

**Pricing:** Free tier with a one-time 125-credit allowance. Standard $15/mo ($12/mo annual, 625 credits), Pro $35/mo (2,250 credits), Unlimited $95/mo ($76/mo annual) with relaxed-rate unlimited generations. Credits burn fast on 4K or Turbo presets — heavy iteration pushes most teams to Unlimited.

**Best for:** B-roll, concept films, ad previsualization, motion design exploration.

**Watch out for:** Credit math on heavy iteration; "Unlimited" uses a slower queue; not a full NLE replacement.

### Pika

[Pika](/tools/pika) optimizes for speed and social-native effects. The current **Pika 2.5** engine adds physics-aware motion plus the Pika Powers suite (Pikaswaps, Pikaffects, Pikadditions) for object swaps and viral-style transformations, and Pikaframes can stretch clips to about 25 seconds. If you need a punchy clip for TikTok or a product teaser without a production day, Pika is often faster than Runway — with less fine control and weaker photorealism on human close-ups.

**Pricing:** Free tier (80 credits/mo, 480p, personal use). Standard $8/mo annual ($10 monthly), Pro $28/mo annual, Fancy $76/mo annual. Commercial rights start at Pro.

**Best for:** Social teasers, meme-adjacent content, rapid experimentation.

### Kling AI

[Kling AI](/tools/kling-ai) has earned a strong reputation for realistic motion and competitive quality per dollar. **Kling 3.0** (February 2026) brought multi-shot generation and native audio, and a faster, cheaper **3.0 Turbo** variant followed in June 2026. Worth testing against Runway if budget is a constraint — it's frequently the best cost-per-clip among the top-tier models.

**Pricing:** Free daily login credits; paid subscriptions from around $7/mo. API access via third-party platforms at per-second rates.

**Best for:** Creators who want high-quality generation without enterprise pricing.

### Luma Dream Machine

[Luma Dream Machine](/tools/luma-dream-machine), now running Luma's **Ray 3** model, excels at cinematic image-to-video — turning a still frame into HDR 1080p motion that feels intentional. Strong for moodboards, hero shots, and fast iteration cycles where you explore many directions quickly.

### Google Flow

[Google Flow](/tools/google-flow) is Google's filmmaking surface for the **Veo 3.1** model family (Full, Fast, and Lite tiers), with native synchronized audio and dialogue — still a genuine differentiator. Image generation inside Flow is free, but video generation requires a paid Google AI subscription (Google AI Pro runs $19.99/mo; the highest-quality tiers sit behind pricier plans), and developers can go straight to the Gemini API or Vertex AI at per-second rates. Access and limits vary by plan and region.

**Best for:** Cinematic hero clips where audio-visual quality matters, teams already in Google's stack.

### Hailuo AI

[Hailuo AI](/tools/hailuo-ai) (MiniMax) is a solid option for fast creative experiments and social-first video prompts, especially if you want an alternative to the US-centric tools. The current Hailuo 2.x models are strong on human motion and expressions, with a generous free daily-credit tier and paid plans from around $10/mo. Caveat: it's a Chinese platform — prompts and output may be processed in China, so think twice before uploading anything proprietary.

## Social Editing and Repurposing

Generative tools make clips; these tools make **publishable videos** from real footage.

### CapCut

[CapCut](/tools/capcut) is the default short-form editor for creators who need captions, templates, effects, and platform-native exports — especially TikTok and Reels. The free tier is genuinely usable (auto-captions, background removal, 1080p export), which is rare in this category. Pro adds the full AI toolkit, 4K export, and bigger cloud storage — currently around $20/mo in the US, though pricing varies by region and platform.

**Best for:** Creators, social managers, fast ad variants.

### VEED

[VEED](/tools/veed) runs in the browser: subtitles, translation, screen recording, and lightweight team collaboration. Less powerful than CapCut for effects; better when you want zero install friction. The free plan watermarks exports; paid tiers (restructured in 2026) start around $12/mo on annual billing.

**Best for:** Marketing teams, Loom-style explainers, multilingual subtitles.

### Opus Clip

[Opus Clip](/tools/opus-clip) turns long-form video (podcasts, webinars, YouTube) into ranked short clips with captions and a virality score. It is one of the highest-ROI tools in a content repurposing stack. Free tier available; paid plans from roughly $19/mo.

**Best for:** Podcasters, YouTubers, webinar repurposing.

Pair with [Descript](/tools/descript) for transcript-first editing if you edit long-form before clipping.

## Avatar and Talking-Head Video

When you need a presenter without a film crew, avatar tools generate video from a script and a face — real or synthetic.

### HeyGen

[HeyGen](/tools/heygen) leads for polished avatar videos: multilingual dubs in 175+ languages, custom avatars, and its latest Avatar IV generation for marketing-ready output. Different category from Runway — script → presenter, not prompt → abstract scene. The old Team plan was replaced by a per-seat Business tier in early 2026.

**Pricing:** Free (3 videos/mo, watermarked, 720p). Creator $29/mo ($24/mo annual, 1080p, no watermark), Business $149/seat/mo with 4K and team features, Enterprise custom.

**Best for:** Product demos, localized marketing, training intros.

### Synthesia

[Synthesia](/tools/synthesia) targets enterprise L&D and internal comms with compliance-friendly workflows, SCORM export (Enterprise), and template libraries. It's the safest procurement choice for large orgs — but self-serve plans are metered in video minutes, and the caps are tight.

**Pricing:** Basic free (10 min/mo, no downloads). Starter $29/mo ($18/mo annual, ~10 min/mo), Creator $89/mo ($64/mo annual, ~30 min/mo, API access), Enterprise custom with unlimited minutes.

**Best for:** HR training, compliance modules, large org rollouts.

### D-ID

[D-ID](/tools/d-id) offers API-friendly avatar generation for products and automated pipelines, plus real-time conversational agents for support and lead-gen use cases. The tradeoff: cheap entry, but tight monthly minute caps that don't roll over.

**Pricing:** 14-day trial (watermarked). Lite $5.99/mo (10 min/mo, watermarked, personal use), higher tiers remove the watermark and add commercial rights and API access, Enterprise custom.

**Best for:** Developers embedding video generation, interactive avatar agents, lightweight avatar experiments.

## How to Choose an AI Video Tool

1. **Name the output.** TikTok clip, training module, and cinematic b-roll need different tools.
2. **Check commercial rights.** Client work requires clear licensing — on several tools (Pika, D-ID, Hailuo's free tier) commercial use only starts on paid plans. Read each tool's terms.
3. **Plan for stitching.** Most generators cap clips at 5–25 seconds; budget time in CapCut, VEED, or Descript.
4. **Test one generative + one editing tool.** Runway + CapCut or Opus Clip covers most creator stacks.
5. **Recheck pricing quarterly.** This category repriced multiple times in the first half of 2026 alone.

## FAQ

### Are AI video tools good enough for client work?

For social clips, internal comms, and b-roll, yes. For broadcast-quality hero ads with strict brand continuity, plan for hybrid workflows — AI assists, humans finish. Also verify the plan tier grants commercial rights before delivering anything to a client.

### What is the best free AI video tool?

[CapCut](/tools/capcut) offers the most practical free tier for editing. For generation, [Kling AI](/tools/kling-ai) and [Hailuo AI](/tools/hailuo-ai) offer daily free credits, and [Pika](/tools/pika) includes a monthly free credit allowance. Free tiers change often — verify limits and watermark rules before committing.

### How long can AI video generators create?

Most cap single generations at 5–25 seconds — Pika stretches to ~25s with Pikaframes, Kling supports multi-shot clips up to ~15s, and Veo 3.1 chains shorter clips via scene extension. Longer videos require stitching multiple clips or using avatar tools with script-driven timelines.

### Runway vs Pika vs Kling — which is best?

Runway (Gen-4.5) for control and pro workflows. Pika 2.5 for fast social content and creative effects. Kling 3.0 for quality-per-dollar generation with native audio. Test all three on the same prompt before deciding — each has a free tier or free credits.

### What happened to OpenAI Sora?

OpenAI ended the Sora consumer web and app experience in April 2026, and the Sora API models are deprecated with removal scheduled for September 2026. If you built on Sora, migrate — Runway, Veo 3.1 (via Flow), and Kling cover the same ground.

### Do I need separate tools for subtitles?

Many editors ([CapCut](/tools/capcut), [VEED](/tools/veed), [Descript](/tools/descript)) include AI subtitles. Dedicated subtitle tools exist, but all-in-one is usually enough.
